# FAQ

<!-- Source: the public FAQ on eduwe.io, condensed. Re-check against the live FAQ before each release. -->

## How Does Pricing Work?

Courses are offered with fixed price or pay-as-you-go pricing. See [Fully Paid vs On Demand](../learner/pricing-models.md).

## How Do I Recharge My Account?

Select **Recharge** in the top bar of your dashboard, enter the amount (the minimum is Rs. 100), and select **Recharge**. The money is used for On Demand courses, and EduWeAi also uses it to give you AI tokens. See [Your dashboard](../getting-started/dashboard.md#recharge-your-account).

## Can I Switch Between Fully Paid And On Demand After Buying?

No. Decide in advance which option you need. See [Fully Paid vs On Demand](../learner/pricing-models.md).

## How Long Do I Have Access?

For Fully Paid courses, one year from the day you subscribe. See [Fully Paid vs On Demand](../learner/pricing-models.md).

## Do I Get A Certificate?

Yes, for courses that list certification. The certificate is generated after you complete the whole course. The certification test opens after you reach 75% qualifying performance in TYS and the course MCQs. Your result is weighted 60% for TYS and MCQs and 40% for the certification test, and the required score is 75%. See [Certificates](../learner/certificates.md).

## What Is Your Refund Policy?

A course cannot be refunded once purchased. For any issue, contact [info@ceekh.com](mailto:info@ceekh.com).

## Do I Need To Install Anything?

No. Courses run in your browser, with online compilers where they apply. A modern browser and a stable internet connection are recommended.

## What Is The Capstone Project?

A guided, portfolio-ready project that maps course concepts to a practical build.

## How Do Academic And Applied Courses Differ?

Academic courses are mapped to the curricula of various universities and focus on fundamentals. Applied courses are industry-oriented, built around real projects, and aligned with the Academic courses as well. Many learning paths pair both.

## How Does AI Assistance Work?

You can generate notes and MCQs and get topic help inside a course. AI usage is metered on pay-as-you-go pricing.

## How Do I Get Help?

If you are logged in, select **Connect** in the top bar of your dashboard to register a complaint. It reaches the EduWe support team as a support ticket. You can also email [info@ceekh.com](mailto:info@ceekh.com). For course-specific questions, use the discussion or Q&A links inside the course. See [Contact](contact.md).
