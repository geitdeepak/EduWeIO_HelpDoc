# Profile And Settings

## Open Your Profile

1. Select the menu icon (three lines) at the top left of your dashboard. The side menu opens.
2. At the top of the menu, under **Welcome**, you see your photo and name. Select them to open your **Profile page**.

## The Side Menu

Under your name, the menu has these groups, each with an arrow that opens more options:

- **Courses**
- **Services**
- **Accounts**
- **Purchase**
- **Query**
- **SetUp**

**Forum** and **LogOut** are at the bottom of the menu.
