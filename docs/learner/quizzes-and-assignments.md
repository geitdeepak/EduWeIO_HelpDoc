# Quizzes And Assignments

Courses include "test yourself" (TYS) MCQs, and AI can generate MCQs on demand.

A chapter's TYS questions appear as a button at the end of its notes, when the instructor has added one for that chapter. See [Taking a course: TYS](taking-a-course.md#tys) for how to find and submit them.

The course MCQs live on their own **MCQs** tab, chapter by chapter, as graded Test MCQs and ungraded Practice MCQs. See [Taking a course: MCQs](taking-a-course.md#mcqs).

Your performance in TYS and the course MCQs counts for 60% of your certification result, and a qualifying performance of 75% opens the certification test. See [Certificates](certificates.md).

For each course, your dashboard shows the percentage of TYS and MCQs you have attempted. See [Your dashboard](../getting-started/dashboard.md).
