# Certificates

Courses that list certification award a certificate. The certificate is generated after you complete the whole course and meet the certification conditions below.

## Certification Path

1. **Work through the course.** Your performance in the Test Yourself questions (TYS) and the course MCQs is recorded.
2. **Open the certification gate.** The gate opens when you reach a qualifying performance of 75% in TYS and the course MCQs.
3. **Take the certification test.**
4. **Reach the required score.** The required score is 75%.

Your result is weighted like this:

| Part | Weight |
|---|---|
| TYS and course MCQs | 60% |
| Certification test | 40% |
