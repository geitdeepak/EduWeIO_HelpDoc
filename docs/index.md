## How It Works

<div class="eduwe-steps" markdown>

1. **Explore**<br>Browse the [catalog](learner/browse-catalog.md) and [watch a demo](learner/watch-a-demo.md).
2. **Choose**<br>Pick [Fully Paid or On Demand](learner/pricing-models.md) and add the course to your cart.
3. **Pay**<br>Sign up or log in, then [check out](learner/cart-and-checkout.md) with Razorpay.
4. **Learn**<br>Land on your [dashboard](getting-started/dashboard.md) and start learning.

</div>
