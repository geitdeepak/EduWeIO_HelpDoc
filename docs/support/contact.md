# Contact

## Register A Complaint From Your Dashboard

You can contact the EduWe support team from your dashboard.

1. Select **Connect** in the top bar. The **Connect** window opens.
2. On the **Complaints** tab, choose a **Category** from the list.
3. Describe the issue in the **Complaint** box.
4. Select **Submit**.

Your complaint reaches the EduWe support team as a support ticket, and the team works to resolve the issue you raised.
