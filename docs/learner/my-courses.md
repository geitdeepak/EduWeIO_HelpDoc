# My Courses

Your purchased courses are listed on your [dashboard](../getting-started/dashboard.md), one row per course. Each row shows the course's Start-Date and End-Date, the course type (Fully Paid or On Demand), and your TYS, MCQ and certification progress.

For Fully Paid courses, access lasts one year from the day you subscribe.
