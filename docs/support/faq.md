# FAQ

<!-- Source: the public FAQ on eduwe.io, condensed. Re-check against the live FAQ before each release. -->

## How Does Pricing Work?

Courses are offered with fixed price or pay-as-you-go pricing. See [Fully Paid vs On Demand](../learner/pricing-models.md).

## Can I Preview A Course Before Buying?

Yes. Select **Demo** on a course card to open its course demo page. You get one selected video and sample notes, and you can see the full chapter list before you decide. See [Watch a demo](../learner/watch-a-demo.md).

## What Does The Code On A Course Card Mean?

Each course card has a short code in the top-right corner of its picture, for example **CLT** for Cloud Computing. See [Course codes](../learner/course-codes.md).

## How Do I Recharge My Account?

Select **Recharge** in the top bar of your dashboard, enter the amount (the minimum is Rs. 100), and select **Recharge**. The money is used for On Demand courses, and EduWeAi also uses it to give you AI tokens. See [Your dashboard](../getting-started/dashboard.md#recharge-your-account).

## Can I Switch Between Fully Paid And On Demand After Buying?

No. Decide in advance which option you need. See [Fully Paid vs On Demand](../learner/pricing-models.md).

## How Do I Pay For A Course?

Add the course to your cart, select **Check Out**, log in or sign up if you have not already, then select **Pay with Razorpay**. You can pay by UPI, card, netbanking or wallet. See [Cart and checkout](../learner/cart-and-checkout.md).

## What Happens After I Pay?

You see a **Payment Successful** screen with your receipt, then land on your learner dashboard, where the course you bought appears as a new row. See [Cart and checkout: After you pay](../learner/cart-and-checkout.md#after-you-pay).

## How Long Do I Have Access?

For Fully Paid courses, one year from the day you subscribe. See [Fully Paid vs On Demand](../learner/pricing-models.md).

## Do I Get A Certificate?

Yes, for courses that list certification. The certificate is generated after you complete the whole course. The certification test opens after you reach 75% qualifying performance in TYS and the course MCQs. Your result is weighted 60% for TYS and MCQs and 40% for the certification test, and the required score is 75%. Meeting every condition makes you eligible to download your certificate. See [Certificates](../learner/certificates.md).

## What Is TYS?

TYS is short for Test Yourself. It is a set of MCQ-style questions some chapters add at the end of their notes. Your TYS results feed the TYS progress circle on your dashboard and count toward your certificate. See [Taking a course: TYS](../learner/taking-a-course.md#tys).

## What Can I Do Inside A Course?

A course home page has nine tabs: **Videos**, **Notes**, **Tutorials**, **MCQs**, **CBSQs**, **SEQs**, **Labs**, **Projects** and **My Notes**. See [Taking a course](../learner/taking-a-course.md).

## What Do CBSQs And SEQs Stand For?

**CBSQ** is Concept Based Short Questions, and **SEQ** is Semester End Questions, written to the pattern of university exams. Both are only available in Academic courses, not Applied courses. See [Taking a course: CBSQs](../learner/taking-a-course.md#cbsqs) and [SEQs](../learner/taking-a-course.md#seqs).

## Are Labs Available In Applied Courses?

No. Labs are only available in Academic courses. See [Taking a course: Labs](../learner/taking-a-course.md#labs).

## Can I Take My Own Notes While Learning?

Yes. Use the **My Notes** tab inside a course, or the book icon below the video player, to write and save your own notes by course, chapter and topic. See [Taking a course: My Notes](../learner/taking-a-course.md#my-notes).

## What Is Your Refund Policy?

A course cannot be refunded once purchased. For any issue, contact [info@ceekh.com](mailto:info@ceekh.com).

## Do I Need To Install Anything?

No. Courses run in your browser, with online compilers where they apply. A modern browser and a stable internet connection are recommended.

## What Is The Capstone Project?

A guided, portfolio-ready project that maps course concepts to a practical build. It lives on the course's **Projects** tab. See [Taking a course: Projects](../learner/taking-a-course.md#projects).

## How Do Academic And Applied Courses Differ?

Academic courses are mapped to the curricula of various universities and focus on fundamentals. Applied courses are industry-oriented, built around real projects, and aligned with the Academic courses as well. Many learning paths pair both.

## How Does AI Assistance Work?

You can generate notes and MCQs and get topic help inside a course. AI usage is metered on pay-as-you-go pricing.

## What Do The Balance, VoD, OnlineIDE And AI Counters Mean?

They are your recharge balance and how it is being used: **VoD** counts hours watched in an On Demand course, **OnlineIDE** counts IDE execution time, and **AI** counts EduWeAi usage, all drawing down your **Balance**. See [Your dashboard: Recharge Balance Summary](../getting-started/dashboard.md#recharge-balance-summary).

## Can I See My Usage And Spending History?

Yes. Select **Statement** on your dashboard to see your free balances and a table of your last 10 days of usage and spend. See [Your dashboard](../getting-started/dashboard.md#recharge-balance-summary).

## Can I Change My Course Rating Later?

Yes. Your Course Rating saves instantly, and you can change it again whenever you like using the up and down arrows on the course row. See [Your dashboard: Course Rating](../getting-started/dashboard.md#course-rating-your-own-rating).

## How Do I Switch Between Light And Dark Theme?

Select the sun icon in the top bar. See [Themes and display](../getting-started/themes-and-display.md).

## Where Do I Manage My Profile And Account Settings?

Select your photo and name at the top of the side menu to open your Profile page. The side menu also has Courses, Services, Accounts, Purchase, Query and SetUp groups. See [Profile and settings](../learner/profile-and-settings.md).

## How Do I Get Help?

If you are logged in, select **Connect** in the top bar of your dashboard to register a complaint. It reaches the EduWe support team as a support ticket. You can also email [info@ceekh.com](mailto:info@ceekh.com), or use the Get In Touch page's contact form. For course-specific questions, use the discussion or Q&A links inside the course. See [Contact](contact.md).
